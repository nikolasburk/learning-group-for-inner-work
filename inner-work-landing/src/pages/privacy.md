### Hosting and server log files

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.). When you visit the site, Cloudflare's infrastructure automatically processes technical data necessary to deliver the page — such as IP address, browser type, date and time of the request, and requested URL — in server log files. This happens for every website and is required to serve the page and keep it secure; no separate analytics or tracking is layered on top (see "No analytics or tracking" below). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating and securing the site).

### Sign-up form (open group)

Signing up for the open group happens directly on this site. You provide your email address, which is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database (see "Hosting and server log files" above for Cloudflare as processor). Sign-up uses a double opt-in: after you submit your email, you must confirm via a link sent to that address before any further email (including the calendar invite) is sent, and before your sign-up is treated as active. Data is used solely to run the sign-up/confirmation flow and send the session link and calendar invite. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into / performing an agreement to take part in a session). 

The site also sets one strictly necessary cookie (<code>iw_open_signup</code>) when you sign up, so the site can show you that you're already signed up for the next session, avoid showing the sign-up form again, and let you cancel your spot without needing the email. It contains the session date, your email, your confirmation status, and the same random cancellation token used in the confirmation email's cancel link. It expires after 60 days and is not used for tracking or analytics. As a strictly necessary cookie it does not require consent under the ePrivacy rules, but is disclosed here for transparency (see also "No analytics or tracking" below).

### Application form (closed group)

Applying for the closed group also happens through an external Google Forms link. This form asks for more detail than the open-group sign-up, including free-text answers about you and what brings you to the group, so that group composition and fit can be assessed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into an agreement to take part in the cycle) and, where free-text answers go beyond what's needed to assess fit, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in running a well-matched group).
