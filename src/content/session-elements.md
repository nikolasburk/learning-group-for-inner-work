## Sitting in silence

"Isn't this meditation?" Meditation is a strong word and it may bring up very different associations depending on who you talk to. I prefer simply calling it "Sitting in silence" for the purpose of this group.

There are no expectations that you've done this before, or that anything special happens for you during the silence. It's simply a way to slow down and observe what's happening inside you. We do it twice: once to arrive, once to digest.

## Check-in & Check-out

During the check-in and check-out rounds, everyone briefly shares how they're here and what's currently on their mind.

This is _not_ about introducing yourself, nor about telling others who you are, or a story about what you're doing in your life; it's about sharing what's happening to you in this current moment. Two hours is enough for that to shift, which is what makes the round at the end worth doing, even when it feels like there's nothing new to say.

## Triads

Triads are the heart of it. They happen in breakout rooms and are very simple: Three people, one question, a set amount of time. One person speaks about whatever comes up for them; the other two listen. When the time's up, the next person speaks. No questions, no advice, no comments. Being listened to like that is a strange experience the first time, and it tends to open things up quickly. 

Afterwards in the big group, we do a brief open round for reflection.
