## Sitting in silence

Isn't this meditation? Meditation is a strong word and it may bring up very different associations depending on who you talk to. I prefer simply calling it "Sitting in silence" for the purpose of this group. There are no expectations that you've done this before, or that anything special happens for you during the silence. It's simply a way to slow down and observe what's happening inside you.

## Check-in & Check-out

During the check-in and check-out rounds, everyone briefly shares how they're here and what's currently on their mind.

This is _not_ about introducing yourself, nor about telling others who you are, or a story about what you're doing in your life; it's about sharing what's happening to you in this current moment.

## Triads

Triads are the heart of it — to me personally, the most powerful tool for human connection I've ever experienced.

And they're simple: three people, one question, a set amount of time. One person speaks about whatever comes up for them; the other two listen. When the time's up, the next person speaks. No questions, no advice, no comments.

Being listened to like that for a few minutes is a strange experience the first time, and it tends to open things up quickly. After each triad, we do a brief open round where anyone can share what they learned or otherwise experienced.
