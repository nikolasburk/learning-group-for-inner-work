
## Slowing down

in a world of ever-accelerating pace, we rarely get the opportunity to pause and reflect what's actually going on inside of us. 

My Practice Group for Inner Work is a space where we do exactly that: slowing down with intention. We look inward and share what's happening inside of us, and we do so in the presence of other people. 

It may feel strange and unfamiliar, but I'm convinced that it's one of the best ways to build resilience and re-establish connection in times of uncertainty and change.
