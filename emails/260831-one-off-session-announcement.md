# Join our one-off session on Wednesday

Hey friend,

after an amazing first session last week, I want to invite you to a <span class="sharpie-highlight sharpie-rotate-1">one-off session this Wednesday, September 2nd</span>. You can [sign up via the website](https://innerwork.nikolasburk.com).

This session will follow the usual structure:

1. **Sitting in silence** — slow down for a few minutes and arrive
2. **Check-in** — everyone says how they’re doing and what’s on their mind
3. **Triad** — we split into groups of three in breakout rooms and work with a prompt
4. **Break**
5. **Triad** — new groups sent into breakout rooms, new prompt
6. **Check-out** — everyone shares where they are now
7. **Sitting in silence** — slow down and digest

This group is an invitation to slow down and connect with other humans. No need to perform or play a version of yourself—come as you are.

I look forward to seeing you,
Nikolas

PS. If you're unsure whether this group is right for you or have any other questions, feel free to reach out—I'm always happy to have a chat!
